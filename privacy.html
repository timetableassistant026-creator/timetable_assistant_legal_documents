This Privacy Policy explains what information the WakatiFlow mobile application ("the App") collects, how it is used, and the choices and rights you have. "We", "us" and "our" mean the developer of the App, the WakatiFlow Development Team, based in Tanzania.

1. Information We Collect

  • Uploaded Content: Images, PDFs, spreadsheets, and audio files you submit for AI processing. Audio may contain voices, which can be personal information. Timetables are often documents that are already widely shared, but they can still contain personal information such as facilitator names. The App asks for your consent before each upload for AI processing.
  • Schedule Data: Session details extracted from your uploads (subject names, room numbers, facilitator names, time intervals). We use facilitator names to organize your schedule, not to identify individuals.
  • Account Information: Your email address and password. Passwords are stored only in a protected, hashed form, never as plain text.
  • Identity Confirmation Details: Your institutional registration number or Form Four exam number, which we use to confirm that you belong to your institution. Depending on who you are, you give either one of these numbers, not both.
  • Information from Institutions: Your institution gives us a list of its members' identity numbers, which we compare with what you enter at sign-up. The list contains only identity numbers, no names or other details.
  • Profile Picture (optional): A picture you may add to personalize your App experience. You can use the App without one.
  • Account Content: Your schedules, sessions, settings, and other content you create in the App, stored on our servers so you can sign in on other devices and see your data.
  • Display Name: The name you choose to show in the App.
  • Device Identifiers: Technical identifiers used for notifications and widgets.
  • Institutional Enrollment and Usage Information: Our administration system stores your email address, your identity number, the institution you are enrolled under, your enrollment status, and your usage counts against your usage limits (for example, the number of AI file uploads).
  • File Fingerprints and Cached Results: A non-reversible SHA-256 hash (fingerprint) of each file you upload, together with the schedule details extracted from it. A hash cannot recreate the file. We keep these to avoid processing the same file again.

2. How We Use Information

  • AI Processing: Uploaded files are sent over an encrypted connection to Google Gemini to extract schedule details. We delete your uploaded file after the schedule details are extracted. The extracted schedule data is kept.
  • Accounts and Sync: We use your email and password to sign you in, and we store your account content so it is available on any device you sign in on.
  • Identity Confirmation: We use your registration number or exam number to confirm your institutional identity and to link you to your institution.
  • Personalization: If you add a profile picture, we use it to personalize your experience in the App.
  • Smart Broadcast: If you enable it, your schedule data is stored on our cloud infrastructure (Cloudflare) so people with your share code can view it.
  • Access and Limits: We use our own administration system to manage institutional access, usage limits, and App settings.
  • Notifications: Reminders are triggered locally on your device.
  • Performance: File fingerprints and cached results let us return the schedule more quickly if the same file is uploaded again, instead of processing it again.
  • Security and Support: To keep the service secure, prevent abuse, and answer your requests.

3. Sharing and Third Parties

  • Google Gemini: Processes uploaded files. See Google's Privacy Policy.
  • Cloudflare: Hosts Smart Broadcast data and related services. See Cloudflare's Privacy Policy.
  • Other Users: Anyone with your 8-character share code can view the schedule, including session details and facilitator names.
  • Your Institution: Institution administrators send us their list of members and subscribe on their behalf. In return they receive a confirmation of the total number of people enrolled, plus the institution's access code and codes for staff and students. We do not currently share information about individual members' usage with institutions.
  • Profile Visibility: The App does not show your email address, identity number, display name, or profile picture to other users.
  • No Sale: We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it for cross-context behavioral advertising.
  • Legal Requirements: We may disclose information if required by law or to protect our rights, users, or the public.

4. Advertising

The App may display advertisements. These advertisements are selected, hosted, and managed by us through our own administration system. At present we do not use third-party advertising networks or advertising SDKs in the App, and we do not give advertisers or ad partners your personal information. We do not use the contents of your uploads to choose which advertisements you see. At present everyone sees the same advertisements and they are not personalized using your personal data. If this changes, we will update this policy. If an advertisement links to an outside website, that website's own privacy policy applies once you open it.

5. Institutional Access

Some App features are available only to users enrolled under an institution with an active subscription. When you create an account, the identity details you provide are used to link you to your institution. Enrollment is handled through our own system rather than through Google Play or the Apple App Store. Payments between an institution and us are handled outside the App and are not collected from you in the App.

6. Data Retention

  • On your device: Data stays until you delete it, use "Clear All Data", or uninstall the App.
  • Smart Broadcast data: Kept until the host deletes the broadcast. Deleting a host's account does not delete their broadcasts automatically. Once a broadcast is deleted, it is removed from our servers and is no longer available through its code.
  • Account information and content: Kept while your account is active. When you delete your account, we delete it, including your email, profile picture, and stored content other than Smart Broadcasts you host (see below). The exceptions are your identity number and enrollment record, which we keep until your institution stops subscribing so we can match them against its member lists, and anything the law requires us to keep.
  • Institutional enrollment and usage records: Kept while your institution's subscription is active. Enrollment ends when the institution stops subscribing or asks us to stop. If you are no longer a member, you are revoked at the next enrollment cycle, when your registration details are missing from your institution's new list, and you can no longer use Institutional Features. If you delete your account, your identity number and enrollment record are kept until your institution stops subscribing, so that we can match them against its lists.
  • File fingerprints and cached results: Kept on our servers for about 6 months, then deleted. Deleting a file, or its sessions, on your device does not remove this cached copy before then. We keep it for operational reasons, so that a file uploaded again does not need to be processed again. This data is linked to your account and is deleted when you delete your account.
  • We may keep information longer where the law requires it or where needed to resolve disputes or enforce our agreements. Copies may also remain briefly in backups or logs.

7. Your Controls and Deletion

  • Edit or delete sessions: You can edit or delete individual sessions in the App. This does not remove the cached copy described in Section 6, which is deleted automatically after 6 months.
  • Clear All Data: Use "Clear All Data" in the App settings to permanently erase all locally stored information.
  • Account deletion: You can delete your account and its data at any time in the App, under Settings > Account > Delete Account, or from your Profile. If you cannot access the App, email us from your account email address and we will review your request and, where appropriate, delete the account.
  • Profile and account details: You can change your password, display name, and profile picture in the App, and you can remove your profile picture. You cannot change your email address or identity details in the App. Once your account is linked to an identity number, no other account can use that number until we step in, so email us if you need a correction or a transfer and we will see what we can do.
  • Smart Broadcasts and account deletion: Smart Broadcasts you host are not removed when you delete your account. Delete each broadcast you no longer want shared before you delete your account.
  • Hosts: Deleting a Smart Broadcast you host removes it from our cloud servers.
  • Users syncing to someone else's code: You can stop syncing at any time. Data you do not host is controlled by the host. To request deletion of other data connected to you, email us at the address below.

8. Your Privacy Rights

You can ask us to exercise the rights below by emailing the address in Section 13. We may need to verify your identity before acting on a request. We will consider each request in line with the law that applies. We do not normally charge for requests.

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, or similar regions

Our legal bases for processing:

  • Providing the service (contract): processing your uploads and schedule data, running Smart Broadcast, and managing your institutional access and usage limits.
  • Consent: where you agree to an upload for AI processing, or choose to share a schedule through a code. You can withdraw consent at any time.
  • Legitimate interests: keeping the service secure, preventing abuse, caching to improve performance, and managing fair use of resources.
  • Legal obligations: where the law requires us to keep or disclose information.

Your rights: you can ask to access the personal data we hold about you; correct inaccurate data; delete your data; restrict or object to certain processing; receive your data in a portable format; and withdraw consent. Withdrawing consent does not affect processing done before you withdrew. We aim to respond within one month, but this is not guaranteed. You also have the right to complain to your local data protection authority.

If you are a California resident

  • The categories of information we collect are described in Section 1, and the purposes for which we use it are described in Section 2.
  • We do not sell personal information and we do not share it for cross-context behavioral advertising, so there is nothing to opt out of.
  • Your rights: you can ask to know what personal information we collected about you and how it is used and shared; to delete it; and to correct it. We will not discriminate against you for using these rights.
  • You may use an authorized agent to make a request on your behalf. We aim to respond within 45 days, and may extend this once by another 45 days when reasonably necessary.

If you are in Tanzania or anywhere else

We handle personal data in line with Tanzania's Personal Data Protection Act and other laws that apply to us. If the law where you live gives you rights over your personal information, such as access, correction, or deletion, email us and we will do our best to honor your request.

9. International Transfers

Our service providers, including Google and Cloudflare, may process information in countries other than your own, where data protection laws may differ. By using the App, you understand that your information may be transferred and processed in those places. We rely on the safeguards of these providers and on what the law requires of us.

10. Security

Data sent between the App and our services is encrypted in transit using HTTPS. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security. Choose a strong password, do not share it, and keep your share codes private if you do not want others to view your schedule.

11. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. By using the App you confirm you are at least 13 years old. Most of our users are university and college students and are adults. If you are under 18, please use the App with the knowledge of a parent or guardian, who may contact us to ask us to review or delete your information. We try to ask only for the information needed to run your account and confirm your institution. If you believe a child under 13 has given us information, contact us and we will take steps to delete it.
